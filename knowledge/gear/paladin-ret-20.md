## What a Ret Paladin wore at the cap (Sept 2026 beta)
The worn set: a self-crafted Veteran's Silvered Chain Helm, three Ruins of Lordaeron drops (Atrophic Girdle, Grip of Fear, Vilewalkers), copper and bronze crafted or auction-house filler elsewhere, and a Rakzur Club in the two-hand slot.

**Four empty slots at the cap**: both rings and both trinkets, in every snapshot from 20 to 25 Sept. Two cheap auction-house rings with Strength or Stamina are the largest unclaimed upgrade on the character, ahead of anything in the lists below. (Beta character notes were removed from the shared repo; only the general gear findings are kept here.)

## The weapon rule
Replace the Rakzur Club (18.3 DPS, speed 2.60) with any two-hander at similar or better DPS **and 3.3 or more speed**. Do not drop below about 16 DPS just to get the speed.

A slow weapon is worth more than the speed suggests, because Seal of Command deals a flat 70% of normal weapon damage on a proc, so procs scale with the size of each hit rather than with DPS, and Holy Strike and Judgement of Command do the same. In Classic the Seal of Command proc rate was about 7 per minute. **Forever's Seal of Command proc rate has not been datamined**, so the size of the effect is unverified; the direction is not in doubt.

Use Rough or Coarse Sharpening Stones. Flat weapon damage applies to every swing and every Seal of Command proc.

## Two-hand targets, best first
Ranked by `https://foreverchanges.pro/bis/paladin` for Horde at 20 [T5 fan-foreverchanges 2026-09-23]. Tier 5: an unverified fan site, no screenshots, and no tier-1 or tier-2 source has confirmed a single entry.

1. **Wolfsbane** — a 2H sword, listed around 25.7 DPS at 3.40 speed on Wowhead, though sources disagree on its secondary stats [T5 fan-foreverchanges 2026-09-23]. End of a long Paladin quest chain: `knowledge/quests/wolfsbane-paladin.md`.
2. **Smite's Mighty Hammer** — Deadmines, which is Alliance territory.
3. **Forsaken Greataxe** — reward from "The Wrath of Rath'mael", picked up in Undercity from level 15. The realistic target, and the maintainer's bridge weapon if Wolfsbane does not come together.
4. **Corpse Chopper** — drops from Bjork in Ruins of Lordaeron. Backup.

Tooltips for the Forsaken Greataxe and the Corpse Chopper have **not** been captured; capture them before choosing between them. Undercity's weapon master Archibald trains two-handed swords and polearms at level 20 for 1 gold, which the Greataxe does not need but Wolfsbane does.

## Other slots, evaluations already done
- Gloves: War Paint Gloves (+3 Strength) beat Bloodspattered Gloves of the Defender. A pair with 8 attack power is roughly equal to War Paint, slightly ahead on damage.
- **+3 Strength is +6 attack power for a Paladin.** Flat attack power only loses to Strength when something scales Strength by a percentage, which for him means Divine Strength or Blessing of Kings.
- Waist: Atrophic Girdle (104 armor, +5 Strength, +5 Stamina, requires 15) was kept over Crusader's Chain Belt.

## Blacksmithing, what is worth crafting
Recipe levels from `https://wowforevertalents.com/professions/blacksmithing/two-handed-maces/` [T5 fan-wowforevertalents 2026-09-23]; The maintainer crafted the Heavy Copper Maul and the Veteran's Silvered Chain Helm himself [T2 in-game 2026-09-23 b:1.60.1.69913].

- Crafter-only upgrades, the reason to keep Blacksmithing: Veteran's Gloves (80), Veteran's Boots (85), **Veteran's Silvered Chain Helm (95, already made)**. The helm is the top-ranked Retribution head at 20 on the fan list above.
- Two-hand maces: Heavy Copper Maul (skill 80, changed from Classic), Solid Iron Maul (130), Golden Iron Destroyer (145), Bronze Warhammer (155), Mithril Warhammer (190, new to Forever). Other two-handers in range: Copper Claymore (65), Copper Battle Axe (70).
- **Bronze Dory**, a new polearm at skill 95: 45–69 damage, 3.40 speed, 16.76 DPS, +8 Agility, +6 attack power, requires 20. Not recommended. Agility does little for a Paladin and polearm training costs 1 gold.
- Rough Bronze Shoulders is a trainer recipe at skill 115 (5 Bronze Bar, a Shadowgem, a Coarse Grinding Stone). Buying the item for about 10 silver instead was the right call.
- Merchant's Favor plans cost 30 gold each at Blacksmithing 45: Veteran's Chain Belt (+4 Strength, +3 Agility, +4 Stamina) against Crusader's Chain Belt (+3 Strength, +4 Stamina, +5 spell power). Crusader's is slightly better for Retribution, but 30 gold for a level-12 item is poor value.
