This is the first file under `knowledge/quests/` (roadmap item 2). Wolfsbane is the top-ranked Horde Retribution two-hander at level 20 on the fan BiS list (`knowledge/gear/paladin-ret-20.md`): a 2H sword listed near 25.7 DPS at 3.40 speed, with sources disagreeing on its secondary stats [T5 fan-foreverchanges 2026-09-23].

## Confirmed steps, seen in game
1. **Diplomatic Incident** — from Danitha Morr at **Bandarion Keep** in Tirisfal Glades, the Forsaken Paladin starting area. Sends you to Trevan Rol at the Sepulcher.
2. **A Curious Pair** — from Trevan Rol. The two "dignitaries" are an Earthen Ring Tauren and a cloaked **high elf**, Lumina Windsinger. Sends you to Deathguard Baldren at the Sepulcher entrance.
3. The Baldren step. The maintainer found one body here; an NPC named Hadrec is also referenced. The step's own quest name was not captured.
4. **Into Fenris Keep** — search Fenris Keep on Fenris Isle, in Lake Lordamere, for the surviving high elf.
5. **The Lumina escort** — contested. Other players start it first and the NPC is then unavailable. The fix is to **join their group**: every party member holding the quest gets credit. Otherwise wait at her spawn.

## Unconfirmed tail — one tier-5 source only
Every line below comes from an mmoexp guide [T5 gold-mmoexp 2026-09-23], a gold-seller site. No screenshot, no tier-1 or tier-2 confirmation, and the order may be wrong.

- Enchanted Silver Ingot — Shadowfang Keep.
- White Stone Oak Lumber — Deadmines, which is in Alliance territory in Westfall.
- Purified Core Gem — Blackfathom Deeps. Pick up Olric Frostvale's quest in Ashenvale first.
- Orrt, an Orc weaponsmith at **Tarren Mill**, forges the Moon-Silver Blade.
- Kill **Old Fire Eye**, a Worgen near the Gilneas gate in Silverpine, using that blade.
- Lumina then converts it into Wolfsbane.

**Cost of being wrong:** this tail needs Shadowfang Keep, Deadmines and Blackfathom Deeps groups. The maintainer is already frustrated by the length. The standing fallback is the Forsaken Greataxe as a bridge weapon, and Wolfsbane only if those three dungeon groups come together anyway.

## Not part of the chain
These are ordinary Classic quests in the same zones that were checked and ruled out. Keeping the list stops them being mistaken for chain steps again.

- **A Recipe for Death** — the Sepulcher apothecary chain, leading to Master Apothecary Faranell in Undercity. Involves a Hardened Tumor and needs 6 Lake Skulker and 6 Lake Creeper Moss from the islands in Lake Lordamere. Rewards: Nightglow Concoction, Acidproof Cloak, Wise Gauntlets.
- **Rot Hide Ichor** — the same apothecary line.
- **A Talking Head**, then **Resting in Pieces**, then **The Hidden Niche** — the Alaric chain. The Hidden Niche is in Fenris Keep's south-west tower: click the dusty shelf.
- **A Husband's Revenge** — from Raleigh Andrean in Undercity. Valdred Moray is at the **Greymane Wall** in southern Silverpine, not on Fenris Isle. Rewards 12 silver and the Ring of Scorn.
- **Beren's Peril** (21) and **The Offering Stone** (23) — the cave is only reachable from the south: take the path around 60,75 to the cave at about 57,70. Ravenclaw mobs, 19–20, undead.
- **The Weaver** (22) — from Ataeric at Ambermill.
- **Arugal's Folly** — elite. Pyrewood Shackles, Shadowfang Keep.
- Shadowvale in Tirisfal, around 12,65: the cellar under the house that is under construction holds a Whispering Elixir, a Glowing Crystal Fragment, Faintly Glowing Bones and Sturdy Lumber.
